# Meridian — AI Infrastructure Advisor for Emerging Economies

Billions of people in the developing world lack basic infrastructure — reliable power, clean water, transit, and connectivity — yet planning these investments requires expensive consultants and months of analysis that most local governments can't afford. **Meridian** is an AI-powered infrastructure intelligence platform that instantly researches any region in the developing world and generates comprehensive, data-driven development plans with sector-by-sector assessments, investment priorities, and phased implementation timelines. Users select a city (like Lagos, Dhaka, or Nairobi) or describe any region, and an AI agent produces interactive radar charts, scored infrastructure gap analyses across six critical sectors, estimated cost ranges, and a multi-year roadmap — the kind of deliverable that traditionally costs $500K+ from a development consultancy, now available in seconds. Built with Next.js, Vercel AI SDK with structured tool-calling, and a distinctive cartographic design language, Meridian demonstrates how AI can democratize infrastructure planning and accelerate development where it's needed most.
